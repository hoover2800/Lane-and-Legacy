Privacy

Lane & Legacy Publishing is operated by Legacy Lane Management Group, LLC. This page describes information handled through laneandlegacy.com.

Email signup

If you request a free resource, we collect your email address, your explicit consent, the resource requested, and a limited source label. We use this information to deliver the resource and occasional related Lane & Legacy emails. Our email provider is Systeme.io. You can unsubscribe using the link in a message or contact kelly@laneandlegacy.com. Unsubscribing stops future marketing email from this signup; operational records needed to honor that choice may remain with the provider.

Website measurement

We use first-party, cookieless event measurement to understand page visits, product views, signup steps, resource downloads, and clicks to marketplaces. Events can include a product, resource, campaign, or broad interest label and a traffic source. The website collector is designed to exclude email addresses, IP addresses, raw query strings, and persistent visitor identifiers from stored events. Browser privacy signals are respected by the analytics script. Aggregate events help us improve pages and resources; a click does not prove a purchase.

Service providers and protection

Cloudflare hosts the website and provides abuse protection for the signup form. A verification challenge may process technical information under Cloudflare's practices. Systeme.io manages email contacts, delivery, and unsubscribe status. We use these services to operate the website and email signup. We do not ask for sensitive personal details through the signup form.

External links

Product links may lead to Amazon or Etsy, and social links may lead to other platforms. Those services have their own privacy practices. We do not receive complete marketplace purchase data from a website click alone.

Questions and requests

For privacy questions, access or deletion requests, or help with email preferences, contact kelly@laneandlegacy.com. We will review requests in light of the information we hold and applicable requirements.
